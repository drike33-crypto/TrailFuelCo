# Top Selling Digital Products: Raw Data Report

Compiled 2026-10-18 for TrailFuelCo.

---

## 0. Read this first: the honesty section

**No marketplace publishes verified unit sales for digital products.** Not Etsy, not Gumroad, not Teachers Pay Teachers, not Shopify. There is no official "top 10 best selling digital products of all time" list anywhere, because no platform releases one.

What actually exists, in order of reliability:

| Tier | Source type | Reliability |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Etsy public shop lifetime sales counters (shown on every shop page) | Real, published by Etsy, shop level only |
| 2 | Etsy listing review counts (public) | Real, but reviews are roughly 10 to 30 percent of buyers, so it is a proxy |
| 3 | Creator self disclosed revenue (Thomas Frank, Easlo) | Real numbers, unaudited, single source |
| 4 | Scraper tools (eRank, EverBee, EtsyHunt, Alura, Marketplace Pulse) | Estimates, methodology not published |
| 5 | Affiliate blogs quoting each other | Near worthless, circular citations |

Teachers Pay Teachers **stopped publishing** its bestseller list entirely. Per Gold City Ventures, TpT pulled it "because the top sellers were getting a lot of negativity," so there is no number one seller on that platform any more. ([goldcityventures.com](https://goldcityventures.com/best-things-to-sell-on-teachers-pay-teachers/))

**Session limitation, stated plainly:** direct page fetching was blocked by this environment's network proxy (403 on CONNECT tunnel). Every figure below came through web search rather than opening the source page myself. Shop counters move daily. Verify any number before you build a business decision on it.

## 3. The graphics: what the top sellers actually look like

### 3.1 The mega bundle collage grid

This is the single most repeated visual format among the volume leaders. HeatherRobertsArt, the number one shop by unit sales, leads with listings whose main image is a **collage grid showing dozens to hundreds of designs at once**, organized into labeled category blocks. The Etsy listing itself is described as "a collage of images showcasing a variety of digital design bundles, with bundles organized into categories." ([etsy.com listing 1605355863](https://www.etsy.com/listing/1605355863/mega-svg-bundle-vol-1-1000-designs))

The mechanism: the thumbnail's job is to communicate **quantity** in under one second. A grid of 40 visible designs in a 2000x2000 square does that. A single clean design does not.

### 3.2 The number in the title

Every top SVG listing puts a count in the title and in the image:

* "Mega SVG Design Bundle: **1500+** Cut Files"
* "MEGA SVG BUNDLE Vol 1, **1000+** Designs"
* "**7000+** Mega SVG Design Bundle: T-Shirt Graphics, Cut Files"

The number is the offer. The design quality is secondary to the count. This is the clearest repeatable pattern in the data.

### 3.3 Mockup in context versus flat file preview

The widely repeated claim is that lifestyle mockups beat flat previews by **30 to 60 percent** on click through and conversion.

**Flag this hard: that number is unsourced.** One guide claims it came from "A/B tests across multiple Etsy shops" but publishes no methodology, and other vendor pages repeat the same 30 to 60 figure, which means it traces to a single unverified claim. Other circulating numbers, "thumbnail optimized listings convert 40% better," "five or more photos bring 2.3x more clicks," "well designed thumbnails get 2 to 3x more clicks," are all uncited. ([ratioready.com](https://ratioready.com/guides/how-to-create-listing-thumbnails-that-convert))

What is consistent across guides, and matches what the top shops actually do:

* Show the printable **on a wall in a frame**, or the planner **open on a desk**, not as a bare PDF cover
* A bare PDF cover "looks generic"
* Keep text overlay **minimal**
* Etsy has **no native thumbnail A/B test.** You change one thumbnail, wait two weeks, and read click through rate in Etsy Stats. That is the only real test available to you.

### 3.4 Color and style in printable wall art

Etsy's own category pages show bestsellers in printable wall art are dominated by:

* **Large bundles in neutral beige and earth tones** ("boho gallery wall" sets), frequently discounted heavily. One 200 print modern boho bundle carries 300+ reviews.
* **Minimalist line art and botanical prints**
* **Typography** present but secondary, mostly short inspirational quotes and "neutral quote" prints
* Rising style named as **"quiet luxury": muted tones, organic shapes, no text** ([insightagent.app](https://www.insightagent.app/trends/etsy-wall-art))

Price anchors in that category: roughly $10 to $25 for digital, $30 to $70 for physical equivalents.

### 3.5 Lettering and typography

No independent study exists on fonts in top selling digital products. What the listing evidence shows: the top volume products are **not** typography driven. They are illustration and file bundles. Typography sells in wall art as a sub segment, not as a leader. Anyone telling you a specific font sells better is guessing.

## 4. The wording: titles, keywords, and copy

### 4.1 Hard constraints

* Etsy title limit: **140 characters** including spaces and separators
* Only the first **40 to 50 characters** display in search results before truncation ([typecount.com](https://typecount.com/blog/etsy-character-limit))

### 4.2 The rule changed, and most advice online is now wrong

Etsy **updated its listing title guidance in September 2025.** Old advice was to pack the title to 140 characters with keyword variations. Etsy's current help page says to "consider using less than 15 words when including the traits that make your item special," and advises against repetition and subjective words. ([etsy.com help](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-in/articles/115015628707-How-to-Create-a-Listing))

Per third party reads of the 2026 guidance, the things to stop doing are: repeating the same keyword, and padding toward 140 characters to fill space. ([listifyai.net](https://listifyai.net/blog/etsy-title-formula-140-characters))

Caveat: Etsy's own help page is the reliable source here. The characterization of what changed in 2025 and 2026 comes from seller blogs, not Etsy.

### 4.3 The working title structure

Primary keyword first, then comma separated attributes:

```
[Primary keyword], [file format], [use case], [customization level], [size]
```

Live example pattern:
`Weekly Planner Printable, Minimalist PDF, Instant Download, A4 and Letter Size`

Actual top seller title, HeatherRobertsArt:
`MEGA SVG BUNDLE Vol 1, 1000+ Designs, Heather Roberts Art Bundle, Huge Svg Bundle, Cut Files Cricut, Silhouette`

Note what that real title does: quantity first, brand second, then the two machine names buyers search for (Cricut, Silhouette). It targets the tool, not the aesthetic.

Another live pattern, wedding category:
`Templett Wedding Invitation Suite, 100% Editable Files, Set of Templates, Invite DIY, Download, Mauve, Peach, Purple, Floral Gold #vmt3124`

That one front loads the **software name** (Templett), then the editability promise, then colors.

**The repeating wording levers across all of them:**

1. A number ("1000+", "7000+", "Set of")
2. The delivery promise ("Instant Download")
3. The software or machine compatibility ("Cricut", "Silhouette", "Templett", "Corjl", "Canva")
4. The editability claim ("100% Editable")
5. The license ("Commercial Use")

Commercial use licensing is a significant lever for the number one shop specifically. HeatherRobertsArt's standard listing permits commercial use capped at 50 units.

## 6. Where they were sold

### 6.1 The marketplaces

| Platform | Role | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Etsy** | Dominant for printables, SVG, templates, clipart | All ten top volume shops are Etsy |
| **Gumroad** | Dominant for software, Notion templates, design assets | 146,271 products analyzed; software is top revenue category |
| **Teachers Pay Teachers** | Education resources only | 300+ teachers have earned $1M+ profit lifetime, per TpT 2022 ([wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teachers_Pay_Teachers)) |
| **Templett / Corjl** | Not stores. Editing layers that Etsy listings sell through | Buyer purchases on Etsy, gets emailed a Corjl or Templett link |

Templett and Corjl matter structurally: the Etsy listing is the storefront, but the product is a live editable template hosted elsewhere. That is how the wedding template shops (ranks 9 and 10) operate.

### 6.2 Etsy's own traffic mix

* **Direct traffic: ~32% of desktop visits**, the single largest source. Organic search second, paid search third. ([similarweb.com](https://www.similarweb.com/website/etsy.com/))
* **Social is only about 9% of total site traffic.** ([shopfoundry.app](https://www.shopfoundry.app/guide/how-to-get-more-traffic-on-etsy))

**That is the most important fact in this entire report.** The best selling digital products on earth were not sold through social media. They were sold through marketplace search. Social accounts for roughly one visit in eleven.

## 7. Facebook vs Instagram vs TikTok vs Pinterest: the direct answer

You asked which of the four sold the most. The answer requires one fact first.

### 7.1 Three of the four are not allowed to sell digital products at all

**Meta (Facebook and Instagram): explicitly prohibited.** Meta's own Commerce Policies state that commerce content "may not promote the buying, selling, or trading of downloadable digital content, digital subscriptions or digital accounts." Penalty for non compliance is "rejection or removal of listings, rejection or removal of product tags, or suspension or termination of access to Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, or Messenger commerce services." This covers Marketplace, buy and sell groups, Page shop sections, and Instagram Shopping product posts. ([facebook.com/policies_center/commerce](https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/commerce))

**TikTok Shop: prohibited by default.** TikTok's Prohibited Products Policy: "Virtual or digital products are prohibited unless approved for sale under the Virtual Goods category." That category is **invite only**. Approved items are software, digital games, in game content, prepaid subscriptions, virtual gift cards, and virtual event tickets. In the EU, digital book sales are "strictly prohibited on TikTok Shop under all circumstances." Listing a digital product as physical to get around this risks warnings, product removal, and account suspension. ([reachsocial.co](https://reachsocial.co/blog/can-you-sell-digital-products-on-tiktok-shop/), [dpl.company](https://dpl.company/workaround-for-selling-digital-products-on-tiktok-shop/))

**Pinterest: never had the restriction, because it was never a checkout.** Pinterest functions as a referral engine to an external store. For digital products that is an advantage, not a limitation.

So for digital products specifically, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok are **traffic sources only**. None of them can be the place the sale happened.

### 7.2 Ranked, for digital products

**1. Pinterest. It is not close.**

* Pinterest is the **top social referrer to Etsy**, ahead of YouTube and Reddit. Similarweb's own etsy.com page confirms the ordering. ([similarweb.com](https://www.similarweb.com/website/etsy.com/))
* **41% of Etsy's social referrals come from Pinterest** (Similarweb, November 2024, cited secondhand, now roughly two years old).
* Pinterest behaves as a search engine, not a feed. Pins surface in search for months to years. Instagram content disappears from feeds within hours; TikTok content has about a week of relevance.
* Purchase intent claims: 75% of Pinners described as in active purchase mode, versus 28% on Facebook and 41% on Instagram. Pinterest's own sourcing, repeated widely, not independently verified.
* Nielsen study cited: 74% begin searching for products on Pinterest an average of **5.3 weeks** before purchasing.
* Pinterest's highest intent categories overlap almost exactly with the top printable niches: home decorating and remodeling, fashion and weddings, beauty, food and cooking, travel planning, fitness and wellness. ([socialecho.net](https://www.socialecho.net/en/blog/docs/pinterest-2026-user-behavior-report-visual-ecommerce))

**2. TikTok.** Highest raw reach and highest conversion rate on physical goods (TikTok Shop conversion cited at 4.7% versus Instagram Shopping 2.1%). But it cannot sell your file. You drive traffic off platform and lose most of it. TikTok Shop US did $15.8B in 2025 with 215,000+ small businesses, and nearly 80% of that is health and beauty, so none of those averages transfer to digital goods. ([socialcommerceclub.com](https://socialcommerceclub.com/blogs/tiktok-shop/tiktok-shop-conversion-rate-how-it-compares-to-every-platform), [wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TikTok_Shop))

**3. Instagram.** Conversion estimates cluster at 1.5% to 2%. Higher average order value ($65) than TikTok. Digital goods banned from Shops, so you are limited to a bio link. Content half life measured in hours.

**4. Facebook.** Lowest purchase intent of the four (28% cited). Digital goods banned. Only real use is Groups for an existing audience, which is a retention channel, not an acquisition one.

### 7.3 The honest caveat on all of section 7

**I found no survey of digital product sellers reporting measured sales by traffic source.** The Pinterest advantage is supported by the Similarweb referral data and by the policy fact that the other three cannot transact. The conversion rate and purchase intent figures are vendor sourced, inconsistent between sources, and mostly measured on physical goods. The 41% figure is from November 2024 and cited secondhand.

Your own UTM tagged data would beat every number in this section. Nothing published is better than measuring your own.

## 8. What the data actually says to do

Stated as findings, not advice.

1. **Highest unit volume category is SVG cut files and commercial use clipart.** Four of the top ten shops. Buyers are other small businesses, not consumers. Buying motive is production input, not decoration.
2. **Highest revenue per product is software and high ticket templates.** $586K to $2.2M range, versus $3.29 median on the volume side. Different business entirely.
3. **The thumbnail communicates quantity, not beauty.** Collage grids with visible counts lead the volume category.
4. **The number in the title is the offer.** "1000+", "7000+", "Set of".
5. **Compatibility keywords outperform aesthetic keywords.** Cricut, Silhouette, Templett, Corjl, Canva. These are searched by name.
6. **The sale happens in marketplace search, not on social.** Social is ~9% of Etsy traffic. Direct is ~32%.
7. **Of the four platforms you asked about, only Pinterest is viable as a direct sales driver**, because Meta and TikTok both prohibit digital goods in their commerce policies.
8. **The distribution is severe.** Median Gumroad creator: $72/month. Top percentile: 99.5% of revenue. Most digital products sell nothing.
